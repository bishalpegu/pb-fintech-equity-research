# PB Fintech Ltd. — Equity Research & Valuation

### Q1 FY27 Earnings Update: A Strong Quarter, Overtaken by the Regulator

**Company:** PB Fintech Ltd. (Policybazaar)  
**Sector:** Financial Services | Insurance Distribution | Fintech  
**Project Type:** Equity Research | Financial Analysis | Valuation  
**Reporting Period:** Q1 FY27  
**Report Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents an independent equity research analysis of PB Fintech Ltd., the parent company of Policybazaar and Paisabazaar.

The report examines the company's Q1 FY27 financial performance, operating metrics, growth drivers and profitability trends. It also evaluates the potential impact of proposed IRDAI regulations on insurance distribution commissions and the resulting implications for future revenue, earnings and valuation.

The analysis combines historical financial performance, forward estimates, scenario modelling and valuation to assess the company's investment outlook amid regulatory uncertainty.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Evaluate PB Fintech's Q1 FY27 financial performance against the previous year and market expectations.
- Analyse revenue growth, operating leverage and profitability across business segments.
- Assess the potential impact of proposed insurance commission caps on the company's business model.
- Develop FY27 and FY28 financial estimates under relevant operating assumptions.
- Evaluate valuation under bear, base and bull scenarios.
- Identify key investment risks, catalysts and factors that could change the investment thesis.

## 3. Key Areas of Analysis

### A. Earnings and Financial Performance

The report evaluates quarterly revenue, EBITDA, profitability and earnings growth, comparing reported performance with historical results and available market consensus estimates.

### B. Business Segment Analysis

The analysis examines the performance of PB Fintech's core online businesses and newer initiatives, with attention to revenue growth, operating margins, insurance premiums and renewal revenue.

### C. Regulatory Risk Assessment

A central focus is IRDAI's September 2026 consultation paper proposing changes to insurance distribution commissions.

The report evaluates the potential consequences for insurance revenue, take rates, operating leverage and future earnings, alongside possible mitigating factors.

### D. Financial Forecasting

The report develops illustrative FY27 and FY28 estimates using assumptions relating to revenue growth, operating margins, regulatory impacts, cost adjustments and profitability.

### E. Scenario-Based Valuation

Three scenarios are used to assess the potential range of outcomes:

- **Bear case:** Regulatory changes broadly follow the proposed framework.
- **Base case:** The final rules are softened, with partial offsets from volume growth and cost adjustments.
- **Bull case:** Regulatory outcomes are more favourable, limiting the impact on earnings.

The scenarios combine operating assumptions with valuation methodologies appropriate to the respective cases.

## 5. Investment Thesis

The report evaluates the tension between PB Fintech's operating performance and the potential disruption from regulatory changes.

**Positive factors examined**
- Strong quarterly revenue and earnings growth.
- Improving profitability in the core online business.
- Growth in renewal and trail revenue.
- Operating leverage and the potential for continued earnings growth.

**Key risks examined**
- Proposed restrictions on insurance distribution commissions.
- Potential pressure on take rates and operating margins.
- Uncertainty surrounding the final regulatory framework.
- Valuation sensitivity to future earnings and regulatory outcomes.

The report concludes with an illustrative HOLD assessment, reflecting uncertainty around the ultimate regulatory impact and the range of possible valuation outcomes.
